Quality Built Into Every Dataset

Consistent annotation requires more than labeling. Our workflow includes structured guidelines and rigorous quality review before delivery.

01

Annotation Guidelines

We establish project-specific classes, labeling rules, visual examples, and handle edge cases systematically.

02

Human Annotation

Trained annotators process the dataset precisely according to the approved project guidelines and methodology.

03

Quality Review

Completed annotations are reviewed internally for missing labels, incorrect classifications, and boundary errors.

04

Final Validation

The approved dataset is compiled and formatted according to the required delivery schema (e.g., COCO, YOLO).

4

5

Final Delivery

The approved dataset is delivered securely in the agreed format.
